## Findings

#### F-01 — Dialog size for ratios just above 1

- **Severity:** 🔹 · **Dimension:** 6 · **Location:** `lib/dialog-size-for-aspect.ts`
- **Observed:** finder 1 F1-6 — orientation is `ratio > 1`, so `"5:4"` / `"4:3"` / `"free"` are width-driven and the
  height cap can win on a short viewport (pre-existing for `"free"`). Documented in Known gaps.
- **Fix / owner:** media-editor, next minor — size by the smaller of the two viewport-driven axes.

#### F-02 — Very long text cards

- **Severity:** 🔹 · **Dimension:** 12 · **Location:** `parts/text-only-canvas.tsx`, `lib/text-card-export.ts`
- **Observed:** below 14 px the input scrolls (≈ 31 of 40 lines visible) while the export centres all lines (36 of 40
  drawn) — browser W2; the documented limit.
- **Fix / owner:** on request (a host-set `maxLength` is the usual answer).

#### F-03 — WebKit not exercised

- **Severity:** 🔹 · **Dimension:** 12
- **Observed:** `container-type: size` + `cqi`, canvas text metrics and the auto-grow are measured in Chromium only.
- **Fix / owner:** the platform's WebKit pass (F-09 of the 0.6.0 review, unchanged).
