### Rotating dimension

- **Chosen:** controlled `post` under realtime + optimistic flows, as the platform will run it (their feed re-renders
  posts from a store and pushes socket deltas).
- **Why:** PC1 is the platform's main ask, and controlled + optimistic + realtime is where reports get lost.
- **Verified?** yes — reported once accepted, rejection never reported, latest-wins, sync handlers, two changes in one
  frame (B1 found here), a post-id swap with a pending change (B2 found here), no-op reports skipped, deltas reported not
  applied, the nested repost card following its prop, `reset` in both modes.

open from the kebab toward the card, LTR unchanged, inside the viewport at 390 px). W6/W7 PASS after the visibility,
