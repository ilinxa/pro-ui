### Rotating dimension

- **Chosen:** native share in a real engine (user activation, hydration, abort, double click).
- **Why:** `navigator.share` cannot be meaningfully exercised in jsdom beyond stubs; the platform's post card ships it.
- **Verified?** yes (stubbed `navigator.share` in Chromium): appears only after hydration, payload has no `undefined`
  keys, abort is silent, a double click during a slow share calls `share` once and the lock releases; without
  `navigator.share` no button and no console output.
