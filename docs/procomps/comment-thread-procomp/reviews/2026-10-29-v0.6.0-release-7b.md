## Findings

#### F-01 — CT-7: the full assembly is heavier than 0.5.0

- **Severity:** 🔸 · **Dimension:** 8 · **Location:** `parts/comment-thread-root.tsx`
- **Observed:** the read-only preview is smaller than 0.5.0, the assembly is 1.53 KB (gzip) larger. The weight that
  stays in every thread: realtime merging, paging, the confirm flow, focus hand-offs.
- **Fix / owner:** next comment-thread minor — move the delete-confirmation flow and the load-more focus hand-off out
  of the root (into the composer / load-more parts or the add-on) and re-measure.

#### F-02 — Focus after a confirmed delete relies on polling for the dialog to leave the DOM

- **Severity:** 🔹 · **Dimension:** 6 · **Location:** `parts/comment-thread-root.tsx` (`handOffFocus`)
- **Observed:** 50 ms ticks, up to 2 s, then one more tick after the dialog unmounts. Verified in Chromium (Radix);
  Base UI's dialog not exercised in a browser.
- **Fix / owner:** re-verify on a Base UI consumer when one is available (comment-thread, next minor).

#### F-03 — Scroll area is always a tab stop

- **Severity:** 🔹 · **Dimension:** 6 · **Location:** `parts/comment-thread-scroll-area.tsx`
- **Observed:** finder A13 — `tabIndex={0}` even when nothing overflows (axe needs it when it does).
- **Fix / owner:** on request.
